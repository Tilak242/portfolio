An agent that writes first drafts of investor, grant and customer documents from the company's live ClickUp, Gmail and Drive context, so a 10-person team spends its time reviewing instead of starting from a blank page.

What I built
  • Retrieval over live company context, so figures come from source systems and not from the model's memory.
  • Drafts refined in passes, with a separate reviewer grading every pass.

AI · Grounded voice tutor

GuruVoice

Type
Voice RAG application
Status
Independent project
Stack
Python, FastAPI, Chroma, Gemini, Sarvam

A multilingual voice tutor for Indian students. You ask by voice in English or Hinglish. It answers only from a verified, curated corpus, speaks the answer, cites its source, and refuses when it can't ground an answer.

What I built
  • Retrieval over a curated corpus with Gemini embeddings and a local Chroma store.
  • Grounded generation that cites the source document and refuses rather than guesses.
  • Sarvam speech-to-text and text-to-speech, with live barge-in so you can interrupt an answer.
  • Follow-up rewriting for multi-turn questions, and a quiz tool that builds a quiz from the last verified answer.

Approach

I treated RAG as something to measure, not trust. I tuned against 86 development cases, then ran 18 new held-out cases once, after the system was frozen, and changed nothing based on the results. I also measured a reranker, found it added about 20 seconds of loading and scoring time, and turned it off in the live path.

Held-out limits, stated plainly: 2 of 11 answerable questions were wrongly refused, and 0 of 5 unanswerable ones were wrongly accepted.
